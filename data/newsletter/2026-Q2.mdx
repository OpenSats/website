Dear Freedom Lovers,

We must build the world we wish to see.

This was the simple premise, the kernel of truth, our north star, that led us
to will OpenSats into existence. An efficient, transparent, sustainable, and
globally scalable nonprofit focused on accelerating freedom technology.
Easier said than done.

The truth is that we were wildly naive on how difficult the task of building
out this mission would be. And yet, here we are, distributing nearly a million
dollars worth of bitcoin to hundreds of grant recipients in over forty
countries every month. Our success is tangible but the mission remains a
challenge, there is plenty of work left to be done.

We could not have accomplished what we have without your support. Thousands of
people have donated. Thousands of builders have applied for grants. The work of
our mostly volunteer team combining the two. We are focused on accelerating
technology, but it is people, motivated individuals, that push this movement
forward.

It should not be a surprise that our pain point is also people. There remains a
shortage of qualified, responsible, trustworthy volunteers. Working on it. We
have also had difficulty securing recurring financial support from both
individuals and companies, many of which benefit from the open source software
we support. Also working on it, but this aspect feels even more uphill. Raising
money is hard. Feels like a treadmill. This is why a large base of recurring
support is so important for our sustainable future. On the bright side, we hold
all donations in bitcoin multisig, which provides us both sovereignty and
purchasing power benefits.

Life is short, the grind is long, the mission is more important than ever. If
you are reading this, do more, whether that is supporting our work at OpenSats
or doing your own thing in parallel to accelerate freedom tech. We need it. Our
children need it. The future is bright if we make it bright.

If not us, then who? If not now, then when?

Push forward.  
Never stop.

<p className="editors-note__signoff">— <a href="/about/odell">ODELL</a></p>

</div>
