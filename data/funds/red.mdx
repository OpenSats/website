We have set up a dedicated fund for people red teaming critical Bitcoin
software. It pays security researchers who are finding and disclosing flaws
in the stack, and it can reimburse the LLM token costs that work burns
through each month.

Applications to this fund are reviewed faster than the usual grant process.
If you are red teaming Bitcoin software today, apply at
[opensats.org/red](/red). The
[announcement](/blog/code-red-supporting-first-responders) has more context,
and related posts live under our [security](/tags/security) tag.

OpenSats is a US 501(c)(3) public charity, and the Red Team Fund runs inside
that structure rather than as a separate organization. Cumulative payouts,
our bylaws, and year-end reports are linked from the
[transparency page](/transparency).

Monthly fiat donations are what keep this fund useful. Token bills arrive
every month, and a recurring gift is how we meet them. Donations from US
taxpayers are tax deductible.
