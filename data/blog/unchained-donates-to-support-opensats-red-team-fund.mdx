We're pleased to announce that [Unchained] has made a donation to support our
[Red Team Fund].

Unchained is a bitcoin financial services company focused on collaborative
custody for individuals and businesses that want to secure their bitcoin and
maintain control of their keys.

The company recognizes the need for red-teaming across the Bitcoin ecosystem
and chose to support the researchers doing this tedious work behind the
scenes.

The Red Team Fund was [created to support] developers and security researchers
working towards identifying and responsibly disclosing vulnerabilities in
critical open-source Bitcoin software. The fund also helps to cover the costs
of AI-assisted security research, including the reimbursement of tokens
needed to conduct large-scale testing to uncover potential weaknesses before
they can be exploited by malicious actors.

the [Red Team Fund].
