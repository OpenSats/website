OpenSats has received the first donation from Strive under its recurring
monthly commitment to support the [Red Team Fund].

[Strive] is a publicly traded finance company and institutional asset manager
with bitcoin at the center of its strategy. In September, Strive announced
its [support for the Red Team Fund] as part of its [Bitcoin Stewardship
Commitment], which also backs open-source Bitcoin development through
[Brink] and public policy work through the [Bitcoin Policy Institute].

The Red Team Fund was established to support security researchers working to
find and responsibly disclose vulnerabilities in critical open-source Bitcoin
software by reimbursing LLM token and infrastructure costs incurred in this
work, giving researchers access to resources that can become expensive when
running large scale security scans and testing.

OpenSats recently announced their [first wave of grants] from the Red Team
Fund, supporting researchers using AI-assisted security scanning, fuzzing,
differential testing, and other methods to examine open-source Bitcoin
software for vulnerabilities.

Strive points to advances in AI that are making software vulnerabilities
easier to discover and exploit. Supporting researchers with access to
frontier models, compute, and other resources helps them use those tools to
find and responsibly disclose vulnerabilities.

OpenSats would like to thank Strive for its commitment and continued monthly
support of the Red Team Fund. Recurring donations provide a dependable source
of funding for researchers doing this work and allow OpenSats to direct
resources toward security efforts across the Bitcoin ecosystem.

If you would like to support developers and researchers red teaming critical
open-source Bitcoin software, you can donate directly to the [Red Team Fund]
with a one-time or recurring donation.
