The [COLDCARD vulnerability] that is currently being exploited shows that
security flaws can go undetected for a long time, even catastrophic ones.

We want to do what we can to help the first responders that have been fire
fighting, triaging, and red teaming as the consequences of this critical
vulnerability are unfolding. A lot of people have been working 24/7 in the last
few days, doing everything in their power to reduce the damage that is being
done.

We see it as our duty to allocate sats where they're needed most. And right now,
support for the red team is duly needed.

If you're someone that has been spending time and money red teaming, please
[reach out to us by sending us an application](/apply/red).
