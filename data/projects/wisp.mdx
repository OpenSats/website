Wisp is a fast Android [nostr](/topics/nostr) client built by
[Barry Deen][barry]. It packages the familiar parts of a social app into one
mobile experience: a chronological feed, photos and videos, rooms, live
streams, private messages, reactions, and a wallet for sending money as easily
as liking a post.

The client is designed for people who are new to nostr. Wisp implements the
[outbox model](/topics/outbox), supports dedicated DM relay sets, and uses
end-to-end encrypted [private messages](/topics/private-dms). Users can join
topic rooms, build custom feeds, upload media through [Blossom](/topics/blossom),
and move around the network without depending on a single platform account.

Wisp also includes a non-custodial wallet based on the
[Breez SDK (Spark)][breez-sdk-spark]. New users can receive [zaps](/topics/zaps),
send [sats](/topics/sats) to creators, pay friends, and recover their wallet
with a seed phrase. The wallet can display balances in USD, and encrypted
wallet backups are stored on relays the user controls.

## Why fund it?

Nostr needs clients that feel useful before a user understands relays, keys, or
Lightning. Wisp hides much of that complexity while keeping the underlying
protocol benefits intact: portable identity, user-chosen relays, open-source
code, and direct bitcoin payments.

OpenSats supported Wisp in the [seventeenth wave of Nostr
grants](/blog/seventeenth-wave-of-nostr-grants#wisp). The grant helps Barry work
on Wisp full-time and keeps development focused on onboarding, mobile polish,
private messaging, and payments.

## What's next?

Near-term work includes [Dark Wisp][dark-wisp], an Android client fork focused
on more private usage patterns, plus [Wisp for iOS][wisp-ios] and private
relay-based communities. Barry is also working on a web client and gift-card
cash-outs through [Bitrefill][bitrefill].
