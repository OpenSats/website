Wisp is an Android [nostr](/topics/nostr) client built by [Barry Deen][barry].
The client keeps nostr's moving parts out of the user's way, like a regular
social app: chronological posts, photos, videos, rooms,
live streams, private messages, reactions, and a wallet for sending money as
easily as liking a post.

Wisp implements the [outbox model](/topics/outbox), supports dedicated
DM relay sets, and uses end-to-end encrypted [private messages](/topics/private-dms).
Users can join topic rooms, build custom feeds, upload media through
[Blossom](/topics/blossom), and move around the network without depending 
on a single platform account.

Wisp also includes a non-custodial wallet based on the
[Breez SDK (Spark)][breez-sdk-spark]. New users can receive [zaps](/topics/zaps),
send [sats](/topics/sats) to creators, pay friends, and recover their wallet
with a seed phrase. The wallet can display balances in USD, and encrypted
wallet backups are stored on relays the user controls.

## Why fund it?

Most people meet nostr through a client. If the app makes relays, keys, and
Lightning feel like homework, they leave. Wisp keeps the experience familiar 
for the user, while still letting them own their identity and choose their relays.

OpenSats supported Wisp in the [seventeenth wave of Nostr
grants](/blog/seventeenth-wave-of-nostr-grants#wisp). The grant helps Barry work
on Wisp full-time and keeps development focused on onboarding, mobile polish,
private messaging, and payments.

Barry is also working on relay infrastructure around Wisp: [MOAR][moar], a
configurable multi-tenant relay gateway; [Haven][haven], a personal relay for
storing, backing up, and sharing user events; and [AlgoRelay][algo-relay], an
algorithmic relay for personalized feeds.

## What's next?

Barry's grant plan starts with the Android app. The first jobs are to leave
beta, keep Google Play releases moving, and make Normie Mode good enough for
people who think in dollars instead of sats. The web client brings the same
account, feeds, rooms, DMs, and wallet flows to people who want Wisp on a
desktop or shared from a link.

[Wisp for iOS][wisp-ios] is available in TestFlight mode. [Dark
Wisp][dark-wisp] explores the same client from a privacy-first angle, with
different defaults for people who want a quieter footprint. Relay-based private
communities are the longer-term piece: small groups with their own relay
policies, their own message history, and fewer reasons to depend on public
infrastructure.
