Someone sat down and wrote that. Probably rewrote it a few times, too.

Our [developer spotlights](/spotlight), [newsletters](/newsletter), and editorial
blog posts are written by humans. The badge is our way of saying so, with a little
room for self-deprecation. Humans can produce slop. We reserve the right to write
a clumsy sentence.

## Why we care

The people we support spend their time building free and open-source software.
When we tell their stories, we want you to hear their voices and understand their
work. The details matter: what someone is working on, why they chose it, and where
they got stuck. Generic praise won't tell you much about any of that.

Writing also makes you think. Choosing your own words forces you to work out what
you mean. We'd like to keep doing that, even when it takes longer.

## About the badge

“100% human slop” is a claim about who wrote the words. You'll still find opinions
you disagree with, jokes that don't land, and the occasional typo. If something
needs correcting, please [tell us](/contact).

You can [meet our content team](/about#content) and read their work in the
[spotlight series](/spotlight).

## Check for slop

Try the [Slop Detector](https://slopdetector.org/) on a piece of writing, including
ours. See what it flags, then decide whether the words are worth keeping.
