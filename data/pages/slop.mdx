There's few things more annoying than reading an article or blog post you're interested in, and after a paragraph or two, realizing that a clanker wrote it all.

Maybe a day will come when the clankers will be good enough to write proper prose. Write something that makes you cry, laugh, doubt, think; something that makes you feel a _human_ connection. But it is not this day. As of this writing (a random Wednesday in November 2026), clankers produce slop. And we don't like slop. We don't like reading it, and we don't want to pollute cyberspace with it. The slopocalypse is bad enough already as-is.

So here's our commitment: everything that has the `100% human slop` badge on it is [written by humans](/about#content), entirely, from start to finish. Out of respect for our readers.
