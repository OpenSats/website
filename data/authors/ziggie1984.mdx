Ziggie began his career as an aerospace engineer developing safety-critical
aviation software. He later moved into Bitcoin development through an OpenSats
grant to contribute to [LND], an implementation of the [Lightning Network](/topics/lightning).
Today, he is one of LND's primary maintainers and works at [Lightning Labs].

His work on LND centers on security analysis and database architecture. He is
driving the effort to move LND's persistence layer toward fully native SQL,
including leading the implementation of its SQL-backed payments database.

Before joining the board, Ziggie served on the [Layer 2 evaluation committee](/selection),
reviewing grant applications and progress reports for projects across the
Layer 2 ecosystem.
