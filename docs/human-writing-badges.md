# Human writing badges

Blog posts and newsletter issues qualify for the human slop badge when their
`humanWrittenScore` frontmatter value is at least 95 (on a 0–100 scale).
The same rule controls the “Written by humans” footer. Missing scores do not
qualify. Use the unrounded human fraction, multiplied by 100; AI-assisted text
is not counted as human-written.

The initial scores were copied from the cached Pangram results on
https://slop.opensats.org/ on November 12, 2026. There were 131 matching content
files, of which 89 met the threshold. No new detector checks were requested.
The website does not contact the detector during builds or page visits.

To add or update a badge, review the article's current result on the slopmeter
and record its human-written percentage in frontmatter. Remove the recorded
score when changing the prose until the revised article has been checked.
Do not infer authorship from an author's name or an article's category.

The badge artwork and explanatory copy on `/slop` remain unchanged. The 95%
threshold is the editorial rule for applying the badge, not a change to its
“100% human slop” label.
